    How it works · fair play
    • Testnet only. Robinhood Chain testnet ETH is free and worth nothing; $SHRWD is a testnet token on vibe/vibe.
    • Where your ETH goes. A free pixel costs 0.00001 ETH. Taking someone's pixel costs ×2 its last price (max ×256): half goes to that player as credit you can withdraw any time, half to the canvas treasury. A new pixel goes fully to the treasury.
    • The treasury. Only the builder's wallet (0x2973…1F62) can withdraw it. Nothing is promised from it.
    • The builder plays too. That wallet painted the hat, the arrow and “Vibe”, and holds $SHRWD — it never ranks in the weekly table.
    • Points are public. Computed from on-chain events by open code (season.js) — anyone can recompute them.
    • Your wallet stays yours. The site only asks for two transactions — paint and withdraw — on a verified contract. Never a signature, a token approval or a recovery phrase.
